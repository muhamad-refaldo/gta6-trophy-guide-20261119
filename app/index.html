Roadmap to the platinum

Six stages, in order, covering all 55 trophies. Play the story first, then the side characters, then the collectibles and the map, then the activities, then the robberies and the money, and finish with the 100% cleanup. Around 200 hours from the first mission to the platinum.

    Missable trophies

    One trophy in the whole list can be missed. The other 54 can all be earned after the story, in free roam.

    Bronze Possibly missable Exploration

    Positive Mental Attitude

    Official: “Meditate at every spot across Leonida.”

    Meditate at all 18 meditation spots across Leonida as Jason and Lucia. The first spot is Lucia meditating on the balcony at Jason’s house, offered after the mission Down to Earth in Chapter 1. Do it before Chapter 1 ends, or it is gone for this playthrough. The 100% checklist only asks for 15 of the 18 spots, but the trophy asks for all 18.

    Sources: KeenGamer trophy guide — https://www.keengamer.com/articles/guides/gta-6-trophy-guide-platinum-roadmap-all-55-trophies/ · Sixshore trophy list — https://sixshore.com/gta-6/guides/trophies-and-achievements/ · GTA Zone trophy list — https://gta-zone.com/news/gta-6-trophies-achievements/

    The other 54 trophies have no point of no return: finish the story first and pick them up afterwards without worry.

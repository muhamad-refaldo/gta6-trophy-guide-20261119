Roadmap to the platinum

Six stages, in order. Do the story first, then the side characters, then the collectibles and activities, then the robberies and money, and finish with the 100% cleanup. About 200 hours in total.

    Missable trophies

    Only one trophy in the whole list can be missed. Everything else can be earned after the story, in free roam.

    Bronze Possibly missable Exploration

    Positive Mental Attitude

    Official: “Meditate at every spot across Leonida.”

    Meditate at all 18 meditation spots across Leonida with Jason and Lucia. The first spot is Lucia meditating on the balcony at Jason’s house, offered after the mission Down to Earth in Chapter 1. Do it before Chapter 1 ends, or it is gone for this playthrough. The 100% checklist only needs 15 of the 18 spots, but the trophy needs all 18.

    Sources: KeenGamer trophy guide — https://www.keengamer.com/articles/guides/gta-6-trophy-guide-platinum-roadmap-all-55-trophies/ · Sixshore trophy list — https://sixshore.com/gta-6/guides/trophies-and-achievements/ · GTA Zone trophy list — https://gta-zone.com/news/gta-6-trophies-achievements/

    Every other trophy — all 54 of them — can still be earned after the final mission. Nothing else in the list has a point of no return.

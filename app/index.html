    Roadmap to the platinum — four stages

    Difficulty 4/10, about 200 hours, one playthrough, no online trophies. There is no mission replay and 15 manual save slots, so keep a save at the start and end of each chapter.

    1. Stage 1 — Finish the story (60–80 hours)

      Play all five chapters with Jason and Lucia. The 12 story trophies unlock automatically, including the gold for the final mission. No gold medals are needed in story missions. Do the Chapter 1 balcony meditation for Positive Mental Attitude before Chapter 1 ends — it is the only possibly missable trophy. Scan vehicles and study animals with the WAiNK app and binoculars from the start: the 100% checklist later wants 200 base-model vehicles and 100 animal species.

      Earns: 12 story trophies + Positive Mental Attitude started

    2. Stage 2 — Reach 100% completion (about 100 hours)

      After the story, open Progress > Stats > 100% Completion. Everything you did earlier already counts, and nothing on the checklist is missable. Work through it in this order: robberies first (39 gang operations, 22 bank jobs, 7 payday loan jobs, 8 bank vaults — the vaults unlock with the Chapter 4 optional mission “That’s Where The Money Is…”), then collectibles (15 of 18 card sets, 100 WAiNK scans, 33 underwater discoveries, 100 flamingos, 200 of 277 vehicles, 100 of 255 animals), then activities (300 of 397 stunts, 70 of 80 challenges, all 42 shooting-range gold medals, all 42 drone time trials, 30 of 40 races won). Skip the hardest leftovers: the checklist is roughly 60% of true completion.

      Earns: A Long Road (gold), Cunning Stunts, The Shootist, Top Fun, Maximum Withdrawal, Pest Control, Don’t Touch My Deck, You WAiNKer!, Whip It Good, One Last Thing, Dime on the Dollar and more

    3. Stage 3 — Strangers & Freaks and side characters (10–20 hours)

      Finish the final missions for Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy and Tyrone, complete 8 stranger mission strands across Leonida, and finish Bret’s 15 Target Deck hunting objectives. Mop up the remaining meditation spots for Positive Mental Attitude.

      Earns: Strangers & Freaks, the five side-character trophies, Fair Game

    4. Stage 4 — Cleanup and the platinum

      Clear the remaining robberies (armored truck, jewelry store undetected), the money trophies ($250,000 laundered, $100,000 spent, all 8 garages), the partner trophies and the odd one-offs (Combat Focus, the Rhino, the VCIA plane). Check the list, finish what is left, and Vice Got a Price! pops.

      Earns: Vice Got a Price! (platinum)

    Missable trophies

    Good news first: only one trophy is reported as possibly missable, and every other trophy can be earned after the story in free roam. There are no difficulty trophies and no mission replay, so keep manual saves at the start and end of each chapter.

    Bronze

    Positive Mental Attitude — possibly missable

    What it asks: meditate at every spot across Leonida — 18 spots in 10 locations, with Jason and Lucia.

    Why it can be missed: the first spot is Lucia meditating on the balcony at Jason’s house, offered after the mission Down to Earth in Chapter 1. It is reported to disappear once Chapter 1 ends. Do it before you leave Chapter 1.

    How to protect it: make a manual save at the start of Chapter 1 and another right after the balcony meditation. The 100% checklist only needs 15 of the 18 spots, but this trophy needs all 18.

    Sources: KeenGamer trophy guide; Sixshore trophy list; GTA Zone trophy list. Planet GTA 6 notes no reliable missable list existed at launch — this is the one PowerPyx and GTABase both flag.

    Everything else is safe

    All 12 story trophies unlock automatically and none can be missed. Free roam stays open after the story, so collectibles, activities, robberies and side missions can all be finished later. Cheats disable trophies and saving, so keep them off during a trophy run — and note cheats only unlock after the story anyway.
